About

A builder by nature.
A teammate by choice.

From my roots in Georgia to new challenges at StockX in Michigan, I’ve built my approach around curiosity, ownership, and a relentless commitment to doing great work. Now based in Raleigh, I bring that mindset to every project.

My strengths span the software development lifecycle, system architecture, DevOps, cloud computing, security fundamentals, and database management. I enjoy connecting the pieces to turn complex requirements into dependable software.

I stay cool under pressure, learn quickly, and build positive relationships across teams. Whether working remotely or in a hybrid setting, I bring adaptability, clear communication, and a proactive approach to delivery.
